Introductions
Hi, we’re Harmonic. We’ve been working with [EXEC SPONSOR] to help craft a compelling, differentiated, and credible story.
So far, we’ve spoken to X, Y, Z, and we’ve learned XYZ.
Our goal today is to hear your perspective on the company and the market.
Do you have any questions or thoughts before we begin?
Personal & Vision
[founder] Why did you start this company? What was your hypothesis?
[outside CEO] Why did you join this company? What was your hypothesis?
 What is the big problem you are trying to solve?
Why is Harmonic here? (Why outside help?)
What would define success for you at the end of this engagement?
What is your vision for the future? Think Henry Ford and faster horses.
Industry & Competition
What’s your perspective on the market opportunity right now? Where do you see the biggest untapped potential?
Which companies might you be disrupting forever? Think Uber vs Taxi Companies.
What changes are you observing in the market? Are there any emerging trends?
Audience & Vision
Who are your most important buyer(s)? Who makes the purchasing decision?
Pretend I am this buyer. Tell me how my life will change if I buy into your vision.
Company Strategy & Roadmap
Where do you want to be two years from now?
From a market perspective?
From a product perspective?
Where do you want to avoid going as a company? Where is the danger?
What has not worked from a go-to-market perspective?
What strategy was attempted but failed?
What’s the biggest internal challenge holding the company back from growing faster?
What’s the biggest external challenge—whether market dynamics, competition, or other forces—impacting growth?
Story
How would you describe the company's story today? What is the core narrative you use to describe your business?
Have you been able to measure the quality of the story?
Does this story accurately convey the vision you just painted?
Where is the misalignment between the story today and where you want the story to be?
Where do you feel the current messaging or positioning is misaligned with the reality of the business?
How do you want your company to be perceived in the industry?
How will you know we’ve crafted the right story for you and your company?
What’s something about your company or product that you feel is undervalued or underappreciated in the market? (What makes you different?)
Go-to-market
Are there segments or markets you feel you should be winning more but aren’t? Why?
Pre-Workshop
Who on your executive team do you feel is well-aligned with the story? Who isn’t?
What do you hope to achieve in our workshop? What are the big obstacles we have to overcome?
Can you send us a copy of your executive pitch deck?
Additional Questions
Have you noticed any changes in customer buying behavior or sales cycles recently?
Who do you consider your biggest competitors, and how do you differentiate yourselves?
Are there new use cases that are impacting your business?
