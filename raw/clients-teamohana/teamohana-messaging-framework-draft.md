Company Narrative and Point of View
Our overarching story explains why our category must exist.


Problem   
The following reflects the top problems faced by the VP of Finance, Head of FP&A, and, to some extent, the Head of Talent or Recruiting.
 
Big Problem
The Headcount Headache

Headcount management is one of the most complex processes in your organization and is responsible for 70% of your OPEX. Yet, it’s still handled by disconnected teams, manual processes, and runs on spreadsheets.



Pain Point 
Untrusted Data  
Lack of Control 
Wasted Time 
Causes 
Scattered data: Headcount data is fragmented across multiple tools within the organization. 

Bad data: Manually assembled data and formulas are error-ridden.

Outdated data: Data is dumped into spreadsheets manually and quickly becomes stale. 
 
Everything is ad hoc: Teams don’t have an effective or efficient process for thoughtfully managing headcount across teams. (e.g., running out of spreadsheets, Airtable, Slack threads).

The wrong tools: We rely on clunky and rigid ticketing systems or other tools not designed to manage cross-functional workflows to manage headcount, leaving teams disconnected, misaligned, and stuck making decisions in silos.
Manual work: Too much time spent pulling, organizing, manipulating, and reconciling conflicting headcount data using spreadsheets to get a basic snapshot of the current state.

Misaligned stakeholders: Teams across HR, finance, and leadership often operate with different priorities and timelines, leading to endless back-and-forths to align on headcount decisions.
Negative Consequences  
No/Poor visibility: No single source of truth for all headcount data. We never have a complete, real-time, and accurate view.

Slow decision-making: I don’t have the data to make fast, intelligent decisions.

Poor perception: Leaders and peers lose trust in your ability to do your job effectively.
Hiring swings: We either hire too slowly, quickly, or in the wrong area. We’re reactive.

Security risk: Sensitive comp and hiring data live in insecure spreadsheets and could get into the hands of the wrong individual. 

Lack of alignment: Weak cross-functional process adoption reduces trust and causes more work/meetings.

Opportunity cost: Our teams are robbed of time that could otherwise be spent on higher-value work.

Costly to scale: The only way to solve the problem is to hire more people, which is an expensive and unsustainable option.

Employee burnout: The constant grind of manual data wrangling and reconciling takes a toll on those doing it.

Manual Work: When teams rely on spreadsheets, they waste valuable time pulling, organizing, updating, and reconciling data. This diverts them from higher-value work and bogs them down with manual, time-consuming, administrative work.

Inaccurate Data: Data stored in spreadsheets is prone to errors and quickly becomes outdated with each manual update. TeamOhana delivers accurate, real-time visibility into your critical headcount data, ensuring you always have the most reliable information at your fingertips.

Data Security: When data lives in highly accessible and shareable spreadsheets, the risk of the wrong people having access to sensitive data increases.  
Cross-Functional Adoption: Platforms often over-prioritize a single key persona, such as HR or Finance, neglecting the needs of secondary and tertiary users—those critical for managing headcount holistically. As a result, teams frequently fall back to using spreadsheets, and gain chaos and lose control. 

Breadth and Depth: While other platforms treat headcount management as a secondary feature within a broader suite, it’s at the core of everything we do. We seamlessly combine the simplicity Talent teams need with the advanced capabilities Finance teams demand.

Configuration: Out of the box, TeamOhana supports all of your core headcount management needs. Other platforms will require extensive configuration, increasing your time to value.  
Breadth and Depth: Most point solutions are new to the category and playing catchup with our offering. They fall short of meeting the advanced needs and expectations of multiple cross-functional teams operating within complex and fast growing organizations.

Enterprise-grade: Out-of-the-box, we deliver the security (RBAC), reliability (uptime), flexibility, and support required to operate at enterprise scale.
