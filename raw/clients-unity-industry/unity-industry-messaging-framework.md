Problems
These are the team, department, or company-level challenges keeping our target audience up at night.
 
Big Problem
The Reality Gap 

The industries we depend on are developing tomorrow’s products in 3D tools, managed by a handful of experts. But for everyone else, those designs might as well be flat files, locked away in specialized software and siloed systems. Without a shared, immersive view into what’s being built, collaboration slows, decisions drift, and momentum dies. 



Pain/Problem Pillars
Creation is bottlenecked by a lack of 3D specialists.
Our development team struggles to collaborate effectively across distance and disciplines.
The rest of the company can’t see what’s being built.

Pain/Problem Statement
Only a handful of experts can build or edit in 3D, leaving everyone else waiting in line for ideas, updates, or new assets.
Cross-functional collaboration is fractured because development teams can’t easily access, share, or review 3D assets in real time.
When non-development team members can’t visualize the product, they cannot contribute meaningfully or prepare to do their jobs. 
Causes 
C++/C# expertise is required to create interactive and immersive 3D. Building in 3D still demands deep technical knowledge of engines, programming languages, and complex tooling. This isn’t a skill a designer or training manager can learn overnight. 

Limited access to C++/C# developers: The demand for 3D experiences has exploded across industries, but the supply of skilled developers hasn’t kept pace. Only a few developers can support multiple teams and initiatives.

Each discipline relies on its own specialized software: These tools don’t speak to one another, creating disconnected workflows and constant translation work between teams.

Files are too heavy to share: 3D files can become enormous, complex, and often incompatible across systems. Sharing them means stripping out critical details, exporting to flat 2D slides or images, or waiting for a specialist to convert them.

Asset versions multiply: Files bounce across email, Slack/Teams, and shared folders, splintering feedback and spreading confusion. No one knows which version is current, what’s approved, or what’s changed.

[repeat] Files are too heavy to share: 3D files can become enormous, complex, and often incompatible across systems. Sharing them means stripping out critical details, exporting to flat 2D slides, or waiting for a specialist to convert them.

There's no way to interact with the product: Without immersive 3D or XR experiences, non-technical teams can’t explore, simulate, or interact with what’s being built, so their understanding stays theoretical instead of tangible.
Negative Consequences

(revenue, cost, risk)  
Innovation bottleneck: When every idea, prototype, or design depends on a small group of 3D specialists to bring it to life, progress stalls, ideas die at the whiteboard, and innovation becomes limited by bandwidth, not imagination.

Lower productivity: Teams that rely on 3D assets (product, manufacturing, marketing, training, etc.) to do their jobs (validate concepts, test ideas, make decisions, create new programs & experiences) have to wait a long time for development resources.

Decision-making slows down: Without a shared, real-time view of the product or process, feedback splinters and review cycles stretch. 

Errors are discovered too late: When versions multiply and feedback splinters, flaws and inefficiencies surface late in the development process, when they’re most expensive and disruptive to fix.

Delayed speed to market: Misalignment and rework compound across the development cycle. Timelines stretch, releases slip, while competitors move faster, turning every delay into a lost market opportunity.

Unique Selling Points (USPs)
Why us and not someone else?
No-code visual editor: An intuitive interface that allows anyone, regardless of technical skill, to create and edit interactive 3D content. Rapidly prototype, iterate, and publish without writing a single line of code.

Extend 3D collaboration to teams downstream: Bring non-development teams directly into live 3D design reviews. They can experience products as they take shape, provide immediate input, and plan downstream activities earlier.

HMI/Embedded
HMI design happens in 3D, but collaboration is still limited to flat files and static reviews. Teams pass around screenshots or renders that can’t show how operators will actually interact with the interface. Feedback is fragmented, usability issues go unnoticed until late in development, and changes become expensive and slow.
HMI teams can now create and collaborate in one shared 3D environment. Designers, engineers, and operators work together in real time, building, reviewing, and refining interfaces as they take shape. Every interaction can be experienced in context, making it easier to align early, move faster, and confidently deliver more intuitive products.
Prototyping 
Prototyping is slow, fragmented, and dependent on 3D specialists. Designers hand ideas off to 3D developers, wait days or weeks to see results, and rely on predefined renders or animations that show, but don’t let them interact. They can’t explore the product from every angle, test it in context, or experience how it truly behaves. Iteration happens in fits and starts, and creativity is throttled by process and resources.
With Unity, anyone can bring an idea to life in real time with our no-code editor. Designers and engineers co-create in 3D, test concepts instantly, and iterate together in a shared environment. Ideas move from whiteboard to working prototype in hours instead of weeks, speeding discovery and unlocking innovation.
Digital Twins 
Creating digital twins requires stitching together data from multiple tools and systems—CAD, simulation, PLM, and IoT—each speaking a different language and owned by different teams. The result is often a static model that looks real (i.e., a digital replica) but isn’t interactive or up to date, so teams can’t rely on it to simulate real-world changes to understand the impact and implications. 
Unity makes digital twins living, breathing reflections of your real-world operations. All data sources connect in one 3D environment, updating dynamically in real time. Engineers, operators, and leaders can visualize performance, simulate changes, predict issues, and optimize systems before making changes in a real-world context. 
Training
Training usually begins only after a product is built. It’s delivered through slides, videos, or other non-interactive formats, making it hard for trainees to fully understand complex concepts or procedures. Because teams can’t train while development is still underway, learning remains reactive, inconsistent, and expensive to scale.
Training experts can easily leverage real-time 3D development assets and use our low-code editor to create interactive simulations early in the product lifecycle. These interactive and immersive experiences let workers explore, practice, and master complex procedures safely before equipment or manufacturing facilities are built, making training faster, safer, and more effective.
Product Configurators 
Product configurators today rely on static images or pre-rendered animations. They show you a version of your choice, but not the full experience. You can’t explore freely, see how materials respond to light, or feel how design decisions come together in real time. The result is a flat, disconnected preview that never quite matches reality.
Now, product configurators come to life in 3D. Customers can explore every angle, swap materials, adjust lighting, and interact with products in real time, whether it’s designing a car or customizing a watch. Every choice feels immediate and tangible, turning configuration into immersion and interest into intent.
