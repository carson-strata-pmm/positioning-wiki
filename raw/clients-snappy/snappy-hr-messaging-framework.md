Problems
Below are the unique pains that your HR customers expressed during interviews with the Harmonic team and third party research/data we found.   

Problem 
Ad hoc gifting
Gifting operations/management 
Recipient impact   
Causes 
Unaware: Many orgs/teams still don’t understand the impact of meaningful gifting on employee engagement and productivity. 
Lack of expertise: Organizations choose gifting opportunities subjectively, without knowing when and what types of gifts can improve engagement the most. Further, gifting trends change quickly, making it harder to find the right gift(s).  

Bandwidth: Teams don’t have the time to invest in gifting strategy, and therefore gifting is treated as a nice to have.

Autonomy: Views on gifting and budgets differ among teams leading to inequitable gifting experiences across a company. 

Sourcing: Finding the vendor and sending the right gift for 00’s or 000’s is stressful and highly manual.  

Shipping: Maintaining addresses and global shipping is time-consuming and stressful. 

Inventory: Managing inventory is done manually and consumes time.
Consumer trends: Individual preferences shift with the consumer product trends, making it difficult to stock gifts that will be consistently appreciated


Preferences: It’s impossible to know/cater to individual preferences across a large group of recipients. 

Dull and impersonal: Relying on generic bulk gifts and transactional gift cards may be convenient, but they fail to convey genuine appreciation and delight a gift should carry.

Impact 
Retention: When organizations don’t give gifts, or gift infrequently, it can lead to lower engagement, belonging, and turnover.

Inequity: Employees feel the disparity from fragmented gifting programs, which compromises culture/equity.

No strategy: When your team doesn’t understand the impact of gifting, you treat it like a checkbox versus an employee engagement strategy. 

Productivity: Teams spend too much time managing gift selection and sending and not enough time on delivering meaningful recognition.

Cost: Undelivered gifts and excess inventory create wasted spend.

Missed opportunities: When your team is burdened by the time it takes to send gifts, you don’t send gifts (or send fewer), which is a missed opportunity to recognize employees. 
Disappointment: Gifts received feel impersonal and they negatively impact the individual experience.
 
Cost: When you give gifts that carry no meaning, you’ve wasted budget and time.

Differentiation

Vs. Transactional Gift Cards 
(Ex. Visa, Cash, Doordash) 
Vs. Other Platforms
(Ex. Goody) 
Instant impression: A tangible or experience oriented gift leaves an instant and lasting impression on the recipient. Cash-equivalent (transactional) gift cards, while offering the flexibility, create a disconnect between the gifting moment and the purchased gift which offers limited benefit in growing relationships and retaining employees.

Sunk cost: Buying gift cards means committing to upfront cost and the max budget per recipient. Snappy offers gift collections where sender’s are only charged once gifts are redeemed and collections have a max budget which means the actual cost to the sender will always be below the budget for each recipient, while still maximizing impact of the gift campaign.

Tax implications: Cash compensation,
such as gift cards carry detrimental tax implications, compared to other gift options

Displayed value: Giving a transactional gift card reveals a dollar amount that can elicit negative reactions from some recipients with different expectations. Tangible gifts don’t have a price displayed on Snappy, so recipients can focus on the meaning behind the gift and not the dollar amount.
Curated marketplace: Snappy has curated gifts in 40 countries, in addition to delivering gifts to 176 countries worldwide, along with dozens of global territories and islands. Localized gifts will resonate more with local recipients, compared to generic goods sold globally by other platforms.

Customizable integrations: Snappy offers customizable integrations to control access to different employee populations in different teams and locations. This enables more granular permissions and control over budgets across a global organization. This compares favorably to more generic integrations by other platforms.

Advanced campaign filters: Give teams more specific control over how and when they recognize different portions of a group of recipients based on any data point. This compares favorably to more generic filters by other platforms.
