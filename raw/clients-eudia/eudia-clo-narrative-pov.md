Context
This document presents an updated point of view (POV) for the Eudia Platform, written from the Chief Legal Officer's (CLO) perspective. While approximately 85% of the original narrative remains intact, this version expands the story to introduce the concept of the Augmented Law Firm.

The rationale for this evolution is as follows. CLOs must first be sold on the value of augmenting their internal team and building a centralized legal brain. Once that foundation is established, the next step is enabling external partners to tap into that same system. Only when both in-house and outside counsel operate from a shared, AI-augmented foundation (i.e., a legal brain) can CLOs unlock the full strategic value of this new operating model.
