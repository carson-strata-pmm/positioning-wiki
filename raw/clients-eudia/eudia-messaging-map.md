Hierarchy and Relationship
For this project, we worked on two narratives—one for the Chief Legal Officer (CLO) and the other for Law Firm Partners.
Eudia’s primary company narrative is built around the CLO audience, representing the demand side of our go-to-market strategy. Our core offering—the Augmented Intelligence Platform (AIP)—is currently focused on transforming in-house legal teams, enabling them to move faster, deliver greater value, and manage risk more effectively with the power of bespoke AI.

To fully unlock the value of our platform, however, we must also build the supply side of the market: a new type of law firm we call the Augmented Law Firm (ALF). The ALF narrative is explicitly designed for law firm partners, positioning them as the future-facing supply that CLOs will increasingly demand.

The relationship between the two narratives is strategic:

CLOs must be sold first. They are the primary buyers of our platform and the drivers of budget decisions.
Once aligned with our AIP vision, CLOs will be encouraged to shift their outside counsel spend toward Augmented Law Firms that deliver more value, speed, and strategic alignment.

Together, these two messaging tracks form an augmented legal network that positions Eudia not just as a legal tech platform but as the architect of a better, more connected legal services model.
