Big Problem
The dual responsibility of providing great care and running a business can feel overwhelming

Each day, you feel increasingly consumed by the business and administrative tasks of running a health and wellness clinic, and it’s wearing you down.

Pain/Problem Statement
The endless cycle of scheduling (and rescheduling), documentation, and billing eats into your nights and weekends. 

Your business has unique needs, so when your tools aren’t flexible or don’t work together, they slow you down.
Your patients struggle to book, prep, or pay for care easily, which adds barriers to them actually receiving it
You were trained to provide great care, not to run a growing business. You're often the only one solving problems, with no peers or team to share the load
Causes 
You wear too many hats: You are the practitioner, administrator, owner, accountant, and marketer every day. 

Constant interruptions: Throughout your day, so many unexpected tasks pop up– late cancellations and credit cards that don’t run– it’s hard to stay on top of it all.

Manual clinical work: You’re still charting by hand or using legacy tools, which means documentation takes hours.

 
Your business is unique: Your tools should adapt to your workflow, not the other way around.
Your practice evolves: You want to grow and evolve your practice over time and need software that can support that.




It can be hard for people to schedule care: Making the time to find and get care isn’t easy, so patients need as few barriers as possible to schedule their care. 

Patients can’t easily access their health information: When health records, messages, or visit summaries are buried in emails or scattered across apps, managing their care is harder than it should be.
No formal business training: You were trained to care for people, not to manage finances, build systems, or scale a business. And now you’re expected to do it all.

The emotional weight of care:
You give so much care to your patients, but you need someone to be there for you when running the business gets hard.

Frustrating, slow, or inaccessible tech support: Other platforms lean heavily on bots or gate support to their most expensive plans, not real people who understand the unique realities of running a clinic.

Negative Consequences
You feel overwhelmed: There’s so much to do, and no time to breathe. You’re stuck just getting through your days, with no space to think about the future.

Work eats into your nights: You give your days to patients, which means evenings are often spent playing catch-up: charting, billing, and prepping.

You’re not showing up the way you want to for your patients: You’re not able to start as prepared as you’d like and following up takes longer than it should.
You waste valuable time: With the wrong tools, you spend more time trying to make technology work than actually using it.
You’re stuck with a system that you’ve outgrown: Your software worked for you in the past, but now you’ve outgrown it. The time and brain space needed to switch feels like too much as you navigate other changes in your evolving practice.
Appointments don’t get booked or rebooked: The harder it is to book and manage care, the less likely patients are to start and stick with it. 

Your practice comes across as unprofessional: When things feel scattered or outdated, patients notice. Even if the care is great, everything around it has the potential to detract from the experience.

You feel isolated: It’s easy to feel like you’re the only one holding everything up, with no one to help carry the load.

You feel burnt out: When you’re always giving and rarely given support in return, it’s exhausting.

You feel stuck: Without trusted support, even small things can turn into hours full of frustration.

Differentiation
Why are we different (and better) than the competition?

Jane vs. multiple tools (ex. Gmail, Zoom, Calendly, SMS)


Jane vs. purpose-built PMS/EMR

SimplePractice, WebPT, Chirotouch, etc.    

Problem:

Limits your potential: Piecing together a patchwork of tools might work for now, but it creates more admin, more stress, and no real path to grow. As your practice evolves, these systems hold you back instead of lifting you up.

Bad experience for you and patients: When tools aren’t built to work together — or built for clinics at all — everything feels harder than it should. Patients get a clunky experience. You get frustration instead of flow.

Tool overload: Every tool is another login, another learning curve, and another distraction from the work that actually matters. Switching between tools adds complexity, increases errors, and drains your focus when you already have enough on your plate.

No real support: Most tools leave you on your own. No guidance, no real people, and no one who understands the full picture of what it takes to run a clinic. You deserve a team that has your back.
Solution:
Designed to grow with you: Jane gives you everything in one place — so as your practice grows, your tools grow with you. Fewer logins, fewer headaches, and a lot more ease. You’ll spend less time managing systems and more time focusing on your patients and your future.
A better experience for everyone: Jane was built for clinics, not cobbled together from tools meant for something else. That means it just fits — for you, your team, and your patients. It’s smooth, intuitive, and made to support your flow (not fight it).
One login, one learning curve, a lot less stress: Jane keeps it simple by doing more in one place. That means fewer distractions, fewer mistakes, and more time to focus on the work that actually matters — caring for your patients and running your practice with confidence.
You’re not alone, ever: When you need help, you’ve got a real team behind you. Jane’s support is made up of people who understand clinics because we’ve run them ourselves. Whether you’re stuck or just curious, we’re here — ready to help, not just respond.
Problem:

It is difficult to adapt to your work style: Most practice management systems lock you into default workflows that don’t match how you actually practice. Customizing the experience—for you or your patients—feels like fighting the system. Jane’s flexibility removes that friction, so your software grows with your business, not against it.

Support that lacks understanding and genuine care: Competitor support often feels like a black box: slow, scripted, or absent. Jane treats support as a core product, not an afterthought. You get fast, human help from a team that listens, understands, and genuinely wants your practice to succeed.

Not built for multidisciplinary, growing teams: Many platforms are optimized for just one type of care. As soon as you expand across disciplines—adding therapists, chiropractors, or PTs—things break down. Jane is designed to handle complexity without creating chaos.

More product than partner: Customers say Jane “gets them”—not just their workflows, but their values. With community resources, transparent communication, and a culture built by a former clinic owner, Jane earns trust in ways ‘big-tech’ competitors don’t.
Solution:
Built to fit the way you work: Every clinic is different — and Jane knows it. Whether you’re a solo practitioner or part of a multidisciplinary team, Jane adapts to your flow instead of forcing you into rigid templates. With customizable settings for you and your patients, Jane supports the way you actually practice — now and as you grow.
Support that feels like a teammate: Support at Jane isn’t a script or a queue — it’s a conversation. You’ll always connect with a real person who understands the clinic world and cares about helping yours thrive. Fast replies, thoughtful answers, and a genuine desire to be helpful — that’s what we show up with, every day.
One platform for all your disciplines: Jane was designed from the ground up to support diverse care teams. Whether you’re bringing together massage therapists, mental health providers, PTs, or chiros, Jane handles the complexity so you don’t have to. No workarounds — just a system that supports your whole team under one roof.
Jane is your partner: Jane isn’t just software; it’s a shared set of values. Founded by a clinic owner, built by a team that listens, and shaped by a community that cares — Jane is the kind of partner that understands what matters to you. With transparency, connection, and real people behind every update, Jane feels more like part of your team than a tool you use.
