Big Hairy Problem
The Scarity Constraint
The traditional legal model has made elite legal expertise a scarce resource. Forcing companies to save it for moments important enough to justify the bill, and leaving many everyday business decisions without the legal judgment needed to grow and protect the business as it scales.

Problem Statement
As the business accelerates, legal leaders struggle to keep pace with the growing volume of legal matters and the evolving legal environment. 
Legal leaders spend too much time on mundane operational work and too little time shaping business outcomes.
Legal efforts are increasingly viewed as a cost to control rather than a source of strategic leverage. 
Causes 
Business velocity is accelerating: AI is compressing the time between idea and execution, creating more contracts, approvals, and legal requests than ever before.

Legal complexity continues to grow: Regulatory requirements, vendor agreements, employment matters, privacy concerns, and commercial negotiations are constantly evolving. 

Traditional legal models don't scale: Legal expertise remains tied to individual lawyers and manual workflows, making it difficult to increase capacity without increasing cost.

 
Legal teams are buried in operational work: Much of legal's time is spent reviewing routine contracts, answering repetitive questions, coordinating outside counsel, and managing processes.

Context is fragmented: Critical business knowledge is scattered across emails, documents, systems, law firms, and individuals, making it difficult to provide proactive and consistent guidance.

…


The billable-hour model rewards activity, not outcomes: Traditional firms generate revenue by selling time, creating incentives that often feel misaligned with your business goals.

Access to elite expertise is expensive: The most experienced legal counsel is concentrated within a small number of firms, making it difficult to engage consistently across everyday business matters.
Legal spend is unpredictable: Legal leaders struggle to forecast costs, explain high invoices, or scale legal support as the business grows.


Negative Consequences






Revenue, cost, risk
Slower business growth: When legal becomes a bottleneck, deals, partnerships, launches, and strategic initiatives take longer to materialize. 

Increased business risk (and cost): Important decisions are made without the level of legal expertise needed to identify hidden risks and downstream consequences.

Missed opportunities: Legal leaders spend their time managing day-to-day work rather than helping the business capitalize on growth opportunities and strategic initiatives.

Team burnout: Legal teams become overwhelmed by the growing demand and constant urgency.
