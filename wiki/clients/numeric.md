# Numeric

## Company Overview
Numeric (numeric.io) is an AI-native accounting automation platform. Series B, $51M raised. Started as month-end close management (reconciliations, flux analysis, journal entries) and is expanding to own the full financial data layer -- bridging accounting operations and finance intelligence. Targets Controllers and accounting teams at high-growth SaaS companies, expanding toward CFOs.

## ICP
- **Primary:** Controller / Head of Accounting -- owns the close process, feels the operational pain daily, expanding role toward strategic finance
- **Secondary:** CFO -- needs financial intelligence and real-time visibility, currently underserved by accounting tools
- **Cross-functional dynamic:** Controllers and CFOs operate with different priorities (operational accuracy vs. strategic insight). Numeric must serve both without over-indexing on either (mirrors TeamOhana's Finance + Talent dynamic)
- **Trigger events:** Pre-IPO/post-IPO readiness, rapid growth outpacing accounting capacity, new CFO/Controller hire, failed ERP migration

## Category Positioning
**Recommended: Category creator.** Numeric sits in the gap between close management tools (BlackLine, FloQast -- not built for finance intelligence) and ERP/GL systems (QuickBooks, NetSuite -- not built for accounting operations). Structurally identical to TeamOhana's gap between FP&A and HRIS.

**Working category name candidates:** Accounting Intelligence Platform, Financial Operations Platform, AI Accounting Platform.

**Three-axis differentiation** (TeamOhana model):
1. **vs. close management tools (BlackLine/FloQast):** Only close, no intelligence
2. **vs. ERP/GL (QuickBooks/NetSuite):** Too broad, not purpose-built for the accounting-to-finance workflow
3. **vs. spreadsheets/manual processes:** The status quo

**Named enemy:** "The disconnected accounting stack" -- the structural problem, not a specific competitor.

**Category trap to avoid:** Being pigeonholed as "FloQast/BlackLine + AI" or "close management with extras" (same trap as BrightEdge's "SEO tool" and Unity Industry's "game engine"). Must lead with the gap, not the origin product.

- **Strongest structural analog is TeamOhana.** Gap-between-categories positioning, cross-functional buyer dynamic, hidden cost opening, three-axis differentiation
