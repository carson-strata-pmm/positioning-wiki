# Unity Industry

## Company Overview
Unity for Industry is a platform that enables every team in manufacturing, product development, and enterprise to create, collaborate, and deploy immersive 3D experiences. Part of Unity Technologies, positioned separately from Unity for Games.

## Engagement Summary
Strata's second-most complex engagement by document volume. Included stakeholder interviews, messaging reviews, POV narrative, and full messaging framework. Authors: Carson, Kevin, Mike. Unity contributors: Will, Ryan, Michael, Felipe, Jessica, Sarah, Marc-Andre.

**Deliverables:**
- Company Narrative & POV ("The Reality Gap")
- Platform Positioning & Messaging Framework
- CEO interview guide
- Jessica Lindl interview transcript/notes
- Messaging pillars review (meeting notes)
- Messaging framework review - 2nd review (meeting notes)

## Hero Problem
**"The Reality Gap."** The industries we depend on are developing products in 3D tools managed by a handful of experts, but for everyone else, those designs might as well be flat files locked in specialized software. Without a shared, immersive view into what's being built, collaboration slows, decisions drift, and momentum dies.

## POV / Opening Strategy
The POV narrative is a standout creative piece. Opens with a philosophical observation ("Seeing is believing. It's how we interpret reality and make sense of the world around us") and builds to the contrast: gaming has bleeding-edge 3D tools, but the industries building "the cars we drive, the buildings we live in" are stuck collaborating in PowerPoint.

Culminates with: "Because if you can't see what you're building, you can't believe in it. And if you can't believe in it, you can't act with confidence."

Closes with a rallying cry: "We're not just powering an industrial revolution. We're leading a revolution in the industrial world."

## ICP
- **Primary:** Head of Manufacturing (VP of Manufacturing, Director of Production Operations) -- focused on throughput, uptime, safety
- **Primary:** Head of Product Development (VP of R&D, Chief Product Officer) -- driving innovation speed and quality
- **Influencer:** Digital Transformation (CDO, VP of Digital Transformation) -- scaling digital tools across enterprise

## Category Positioning
Category redefiner. Repositions from "game engine for industry" to "the platform where every team can create and collaborate in a rich and immersive 3D environment." Broadens the audience from specialized developers to all stakeholders.

Differentiates on three axes:
1. **vs. CAD solutions (Autodesk, Siemens):** Open, format-agnostic, enables more people to create, integrates with existing systems
2. **vs. real-time 3D editors (Unreal):** No-code editor, enterprise-grade support, more industry experience, wider adoption
3. **vs. DAMs (Echo3D, Helix, Adobe):** Creates interactive experiences (not just stores assets), deep integration with industrial systems

## Key Messaging Pillars
1. **Enable more people to create in 3D** -- no-code editor, AI-driven creation, lower the expertise barrier
2. **One place where every team can collaborate in 3D** -- format-agnostic, web-based, single source of truth
3. **Put immersive experiences in everyone's hands** -- deploy to any device, extend 3D to downstream teams

## Proof Point Types Used
- **Use cases (before/after):** HMI/embedded, prototyping, digital twins, training, product configurators
- **Capability claims:** 70+ file format support, web-based access, AI-built-in, deploy anywhere
- **No named customer metrics** in the framework

## Lessons & Patterns
- **The POV narrative is the most literary.** "Seeing is believing" and "The Reality Gap" work as a narrative because they tap into something universal -- the gap between what we can imagine and what we can share.
- **Meeting notes reveal the process.** The pillars review and framework review transcripts show how messaging evolved through collaborative refinement with the client team.
- **"Democratize creation" is the core value bet.** The no-code editor is the linchpin: if more people can create in 3D, the entire value proposition unlocks.
- **Three-axis differentiation** mirrors TeamOhana's approach -- structured competitive mapping that addresses distinct alternative categories.
- **The keynote connection.** Interview notes reveal the framework was being developed alongside an AES keynote, showing how messaging frameworks feed into executive presentations.
