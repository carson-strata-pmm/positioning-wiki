# General Legal

## Company Overview
General Legal is an AI-native law firm that combines elite attorneys (top law school graduates, X+ years at top firms) with an AI-native operating model to scale legal judgment for growing companies at 1/5 the cost of traditional firms. Authors: Carson, Mike, Kevin.

## Engagement Summary
Strata delivered a Platform Positioning & Messaging Framework.

**Deliverables:**
- Messaging Framework

## Hero Problem
**"The Scarcity Constraint."** The traditional legal model has made elite legal expertise a scarce resource, forcing companies to reserve it for moments important enough to justify the bill. Everyday business decisions go without the legal judgment needed to grow and protect the company.

## POV / Opening Strategy
Indicts the traditional legal model's economics: billable hours reward activity not outcomes, elite expertise is concentrated in a few expensive firms, and legal spend is unpredictable. Invokes a future state where "legal becomes a force multiplier for company growth" rather than a cost center.

## ICP
- **Primary (Scale segment):** General Counsel / Head of Legal at scaling companies -- facing mounting legal demand without proportional resources, consumed by operational work
- **Secondary (Growth segment):** Founder / CEO at early-stage companies -- needs expert guidance on fundraising, hiring, contracts, but can't justify in-house legal or traditional firm costs

## Category Positioning
Category redefiner. Takes the "law firm" category and redefines it as an "AI-Native Law Firm." The model inverts the traditional firm: senior attorneys (not junior associates) do the work, supported by AI that handles coordination, information retrieval, and routine tasks. Flat-fee pricing replaces hourly billing.

Differentiates vs. AI-native solutions (Crosby, Arcline) on elite legal talent and full-stack expertise. Differentiates vs. traditional firms (Kirkland, Latham, Cooley) on embedded access, AI-native throughput, predictable economics, and compounding context.

## Key Messaging Pillars
1. **Elite legal expertise** -- access attorneys with judgment typically reserved for the world's largest companies
2. **AI-native legal operations** -- technology handles coordination and routine work, freeing attorneys for more decisions
3. **Contextualized legal judgment** -- every interaction deepens understanding; advice becomes more tailored over time

## Proof Point Types Used
- **Proof point gap:** Validation section is empty -- no case studies, customer metrics, or quotes in the framework
- **USP claims:** Proven credentials, faster turnaround times, predictable pricing, compounding judgment, market intelligence

## Lessons & Patterns
- "Compounding judgment" and "institutional intelligence" are compelling concepts: the idea that every engagement makes the next one better. This is a reusable pattern for any AI-native service business.
- "Market intelligence" as a USP (patterns drawn from deals across industries) positions the firm as an intelligence platform, not just a service provider.
- The "Aligned incentives" differentiation point is powerful: "We don't win by billing hours. We win by building a lasting relationship."
- Notable overlap with Eudia's Augmented Law Firm concept -- General Legal is essentially a standalone ALF.
- Stage-based segmentation (Scale vs. Growth) is cleaner than vertical segmentation for legal services.
