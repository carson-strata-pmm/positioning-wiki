# Eudia

## Company Overview
Eudia is an Augmented Intelligence Platform (AIP) for corporate legal teams. The platform combines bespoke AI workflows with human expertise to augment in-house legal teams and their outside counsel network. Also created the "Augmented Law Firm" (ALF) category for a new type of AI-native outside counsel.

## Engagement Summary
Strata's most complex engagement, producing dual narratives for two sides of a marketplace: CLOs (demand) and law firm partners (supply). Authors: Carson, Mike, Kevin.

**Deliverables:**
- CLO Strategic Narrative & POV (two versions -- original and updated with ALF)
- CLO Messaging Framework
- Augmented Law Firm POV (partner-facing)
- Augmented Law Firm Messaging Framework
- Messaging Map (hierarchy document explaining how all pieces fit together)
- Earlier "ALF messaging old" draft

## Hero Problem
**"The speed-to-risk ratio."** Legal sits between two conflicting demands: operating at the speed of the business and protecting it. This ratio has become unsustainable as matter volumes rise, budgets stay flat, and AI accelerates business pace. For ALF partners: **"Disruption of my career"** -- the system partners sacrificed everything for has an expiration date.

## POV / Opening Strategy
The most sophisticated POV across all engagements. Opens with rhythmic repetition ("Speed. Risk. Speed. Risk. Speed. Risk.") to physically embody the tension. Combines three opening strategies:
1. **Indict the status quo:** Old solutions (people, software, outside counsel) all come with trade-offs
2. **Name a paradox:** Speed and risk are in permanent tension
3. **Invoke an inevitable future:** "You can lead the way or be left behind"

Also introduces the "Iron Man suit" metaphor: "It's like outfitting every attorney in your network with an Iron Man suit."

## ICP
- **CLO Narrative (demand side):**
  - Primary: CLO / Head of Legal (economic buyer, decision maker)
  - Secondary: Deputy GC / Head of Division Legal
  - Influencer: Head of Legal Ops
- **ALF Narrative (supply side):**
  - Primary: Law Firm Partner (Managing Partner, Equity Partner)
  - Secondary: Head of Legal (CLO) -- also appears on demand side

## Category Positioning
**Dual category creator:**
1. **Augmented Intelligence Platform (AIP)** -- distinguished from "artificial intelligence" (off-the-shelf AI). The name emphasizes human augmentation, not replacement.
2. **Augmented Law Firm (ALF)** -- a new category of law firm rebuilt from the ground up with AI. Positioned against Traditional Law Firms across 10 dimensions (pricing, staffing, technology, culture, etc.).

The "Augmented" branding is deliberately chosen for consistency (Augmented Intelligence Platform + Augmented Law Firm) and future scalability (could extend to "Augmented Accounting Firm," etc.).

## Key Messaging Pillars
**CLO Framework:**
1. **Deliver greater business value** -- surface insights from the "legal brain" to proactively add value
2. **Supercharge your network of attorneys** -- scale capacity without adding expensive resources
3. **Stop the toil** -- automate mundane work so lawyers focus on high-value practice

**ALF Framework:**
1. **Provide greater client value** -- align pricing and delivery with client expectations
2. **Agency over my practice** -- work creatively, avoid burnout, focus on meaningful work
3. **Shape the future** -- leverage AI as a core capability, not a bolt-on

## Proof Point Types Used
- **Financial ROI:** $30M+ in contract enforcement opportunities, ~$10M top-line from risk quantification, displacing ~$10M retainer
- **Industry stats:** "79% of legal teams reported an increase in matter volume"
- **Use case scenarios:** Contract drafting, risk review, redlining, due diligence, compliance, privacy, IP, value creation insights (detailed "today vs. AI vs. augmented intelligence" comparison)
- **Differentiation table:** Traditional Law Firm vs. Augmented Law Firm (10 dimensions)

## Lessons & Patterns
- **Dual-narrative architecture is unique.** The CLO and ALF narratives are strategically sequenced: "CLOs must be sold first" before shifting outside counsel spend to ALFs.
- **The Messaging Map is a meta-deliverable.** It explains how documents relate to each other, useful for complex engagements with multiple audiences.
- **Brand voice is explicitly defined.** "Academic, insider, inventive, premium, communal, trustworthy" -- reflects that lawyers are skeptical buyers who need domain-credible language.
- **Category naming is deeply intentional.** "Augmented Intelligence" was chosen over "Artificial Intelligence" to emphasize human-centric AI and future scalability.
- **The "10x attorney" concept** positions AI-augmented lawyers as dramatically more productive, similar to the "10x engineer" archetype.
- **Mission statement included:** "We exist to enable humanity to achieve its highest good through Augmented Intelligence" -- rare for a messaging framework to include a mission.
