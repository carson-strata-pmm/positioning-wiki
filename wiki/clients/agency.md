# Agency

## Company Overview
Agency builds Kai, a "superintelligent customer AI agent" -- a fully autonomous AI co-worker that manages onboarding, support, and success across an entire customer base. Targets AI-native startups through enterprise.

## Engagement Summary
Strata delivered a Platform Positioning & Messaging Framework and a narrative thinking document (early strategic ideation). Authors: Carson, Mike, Kevin.

**Deliverables:**
- Messaging Framework
- Narrative thinking document (strategic exploration)

## Hero Problem
**"The Customer Value Paradox" / "The Bureaucracy Bottleneck."** The irony: companies reflexively hire more people to serve customers as they grow, but more people create more handoffs, delays, and friction, actually degrading the customer experience. Framed as a paradox that indicts the human-led CX model.

Earlier exploration also named it "Humans are the customer experience bottleneck" and "Customer experience doesn't scale with headcount."

## POV / Opening Strategy
Paradox-driven opening: names the contradiction in how companies scale customer success. Also invokes an inevitable future ("The future of customer success isn't more people -- it's Kai"). Draws a clear line between "bolt-on AI" (layered on broken human workflows) and "AI-native" (rebuilt from the ground up).

## ICP
- **Primary (early):** CEO of AI-native startups -- builds around AI from day one, fears losing customer intimacy at scale
- **Secondary (mid-market):** CRO -- focused on revenue leakage, operational leverage, margin preservation
- **Tertiary (enterprise):** CCO -- responsible for post-sale CX, GRR/NRR, but often risk-averse and protective of legacy teams
- **Strategic target:** Enterprise "long tail" -- the 80-90% of customers often ignored by legacy success teams

## Category Positioning
Category creator. Positions Kai as the first "superintelligent customer AI agent" -- not a copilot, not an assistant, but full autonomous ownership of CX. "Autopilot, not copilot." This is described as a "strategic hill to die on."

Differentiates against two false premises: (1) future CX will remain human-led, and (2) AI agents should be point solutions for individual functions.

## Key Messaging Pillars
1. **Single point of contact** -- replaces layers of people with one consistent, trusted AI interface
2. **Complete context on every customer** -- unmatched customer knowledge no human team could achieve
3. **Infinite capacity to serve** -- continuously adapts and engages without burnout or limits

## Proof Point Types Used
- **Use cases (before/after):** Onboarding, service, success
- **Proof point gap:** No named customers or hard metrics in the framework. Marked TBD for Agency to complete.

## Lessons & Patterns
- The narrative thinking document shows the strategic ideation process: naming the problem, labeling the old approach, identifying de-positioning targets
- "Bolt-on AI vs. AI-native" is a powerful conceptual fork that could apply to many AI-first clients
- The "long tail" opportunity (underserved 80-90% of enterprise customers) is a reusable ICP framing
- "Compound company" vision (like Rippling) positions Agency as a platform, not a point product
- Founder's intuition ("Designed by Elias") as a USP is unusual -- personalizes the AI's judgment
- Framework has proof point gaps -- needs customer validation data
