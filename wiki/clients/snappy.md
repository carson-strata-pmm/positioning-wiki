# Snappy

## Company Overview
Snappy is an employee gifting platform for HR teams. Enables companies to build, automate, and scale gifting campaigns across milestones, onboarding, and recognition moments. Global marketplace with gifts in 40+ countries.

## Engagement Summary
Strata delivered an HR Messaging Framework focused on the employee gifting use case. Described as an internal messaging strategy document (not customer-facing copy). Authors: Carson, Mike.

**Deliverables:**
- HR Messaging Framework

## Hero Problem
**"Ad hoc gifting."** Organizations treat gifting as a checkbox rather than a strategic employee engagement tool. Teams lack expertise on when and what types of gifts improve engagement, don't have bandwidth for strategy, and create inequitable experiences across the company.

## POV / Opening Strategy
Frames gifting as an underinvested part of employee recognition strategy. Positions the gap between knowing that recognition matters and actually executing meaningful gifting at scale. The implicit message: "Employees don't just need to hear 'thank you,' they need to feel 'thank you.'"

## ICP
- **Primary:** HR Leader -- aiming to elevate employee engagement but struggling to implement high-impact gifting campaigns
- **Buying dynamic:** Teams don't have time to invest in gifting strategy, so it's treated as nice-to-have

## Category Positioning
Head-to-head competitor. Differentiates on two axes:
1. **vs. transactional gift cards** (Visa, Cash, DoorDash): Tangible gifts create lasting impressions; no sunk cost (pay on redemption only); no revealed dollar amount; no tax implications
2. **vs. other platforms** (Goody): Curated global marketplace (40+ countries), customizable integrations with HRIS, advanced campaign filters

## Key Messaging Pillars
1. **Expertise** -- easy for HR teams to design and automate unique gifting campaigns
2. **Scale** -- automates the entire gifting process with triggers and milestones
3. **Delight** -- every gift is personal and meaningful, unlocking genuine gratitude

## Proof Point Types Used
- **Third-party research:** "14% higher engagement/productivity in organizations with recognition," "61% of employees believe employers who give gifts are more thoughtful," "81% say gifts make them feel appreciated"
- **No named customer metrics or case studies**

## Lessons & Patterns
- **Smallest scope engagement.** Single-persona, single use case, no POV narrative. The framework is positioned as an internal strategy document, not a full messaging system.
- **Terminology hierarchy is interesting:** Platform level focuses on relationships (employees, customers, partners); HR level narrows to employee engagement and recognition. Gifting is positioned as part of a complete recognition strategy.
- **"Feel 'thank you'" is strong language capture.** Moves gifting from transactional to emotional.
- **Pay-on-redemption pricing** is a strong differentiator against gift cards where budget is committed upfront.
