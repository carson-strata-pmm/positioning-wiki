# Avoca

## Company Overview
Avoca is an agentic platform for home services businesses that deploys AI agents to handle inbound calls, lead follow-up, outbound campaigns, and scheduling across the customer lifecycle.

## Engagement Summary
Strata delivered a full Platform Positioning & Messaging Framework, co-founder interview guide, and a messaging playground (early ideation). Authors: Carson, Mike, Kevin.

**Deliverables:**
- Messaging Framework (final)
- Co-founder interview guide
- Messaging playground (frameworks)

## Hero Problem
**"Your growth is hitting a ceiling."** Services businesses leak revenue at every stage of the customer journey -- missed calls, forgotten follow-ups, unworked estimates. Every miss is a lead handed to a competitor, and most operators don't see the full cost until growth stalls. Framed as compounding operational failure.

## POV / Opening Strategy
Indicts the status quo by surfacing the hidden costs of CSR instability (~$46K per churn event) and response gaps ("90% of leads are gone after 20 seconds"). Positions the front office as "the most important function in the business" running on "the most volatile workforce in the industry."

## ICP
- **Primary:** Owner-Operator (CEO, GM, President) -- responsible for overall performance, focused on capturing demand and maximizing technician productivity
- **Secondary:** Customer Service Leader (Director of Customer Services) -- manages call center performance, staffing, coaching
- **Tertiary:** Digital Transformation Lead (CTO) -- PE-backed portfolios standardizing tech across multi-location brands

## Category Positioning
Head-to-head competitor positioning. Differentiates vs. point solutions (Broccoli, Netic) on end-to-end lifecycle coverage, enterprise readiness, and human-in-the-loop. Differentiates vs. status quo (human teams, call centers) on reliability, measurability, and scalability.

## Key Messaging Pillars
1. **Book more jobs** -- ensure every call, lead, and follow-up is handled instantly
2. **Deliver exceptional experiences** -- consistent, high-quality interactions regardless of time or channel
3. **Scale your front office** -- run customer operations without headcount constraints

## Proof Point Types Used
- **Financial ROI:** 13% revenue growth (My Plumber Plus), 8.6x increase in bookings (Aire Serv), $800K from single branch outbound campaign
- **Operational metrics:** 75% booking rate, ~90% AI-handled calls, <3 second answer time, 0 min hold time, near-zero abandonment
- **Before/after use cases:** Inbound capture, lead follow-up, nurture campaigns, scheduling, after-hours

## Lessons & Patterns
- Strong example of the 3-pillar symmetric architecture (problem > solution > outcome)
- Rich proof point library with named customers -- strongest across all engagements
- Owner-operator persona requires empathetic, non-corporate language ("When something breaks, customers don't wait")
- Use case narratives are exceptionally detailed and sales-ready
- The messaging playground shows early ideation of the "big hairy problem" concept before it became "your growth is hitting a ceiling"
