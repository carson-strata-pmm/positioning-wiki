# TeamOhana

## Company Overview
TeamOhana is a headcount management platform that unites Finance, HR, and Talent teams on a single solution for planning, managing, and optimizing headcount spend. Targets VC-backed startups through enterprise.

## Engagement Summary
Strata delivered a Platform Positioning & Messaging Framework through multiple iterations. Extensive copy bank with many elevator pitch alternatives.

**Deliverables:**
- Messaging Framework (final)
- Messaging Framework (draft with scratch space and alternatives)

## Hero Problem
**"The Headcount Headache."** Headcount management is one of the most complex processes in an organization and responsible for 70% of OPEX, yet it's still handled by disconnected teams, manual processes, and spreadsheets. Framed as operational failure with outsized financial consequence.

## POV / Opening Strategy
Surfaces a hidden cost: 70% of operating expenditure is managed through spreadsheets with untrusted data, no control, and wasted time. The slogan "End the headcount headache" positions TeamOhana as the cure for a universal, named pain.

## ICP
- **Primary:** VP of Finance / Head of FP&A -- needs accurate data for fast decisions, controls budget
- **Secondary:** Head of Talent / Recruiting -- needs a tool that works for hiring workflows
- **Cross-functional dynamic:** The key challenge is that Finance, HR, and Talent teams operate with different priorities and tools, creating misalignment

## Category Positioning
Category creator. TeamOhana is positioned as "the first and only headcount management platform." Traditional financial software wasn't built for recruiting; recruiting software doesn't meet finance data requirements. TeamOhana bridges the gap.

Differentiates on three axes:
1. **vs. spreadsheets:** Real-time accuracy, data security, elimination of manual work
2. **vs. platforms (FP&A or HRIS):** Cross-functional adoption, breadth and depth, minimal configuration
3. **vs. point solutions (Headcount365, CandorIQ, Aura):** Enterprise-grade, broader feature set, more mature

## Key Messaging Pillars
1. **Real-time visibility** -- single, accurate source of truth for all headcount data
2. **Unified process** -- align all teams and improve collaboration
3. **Automated workflows** -- eliminate manual tasks, free teams for high-value work

## Proof Point Types Used
- **Financial ROI:** $294K average savings in unapproved headcount costs, prevented $200K in unapproved costs
- **Productivity metrics:** 75% reduction in manual work, 60 hours saved monthly (25% of the month)
- **Customer quote:** Extended testimonial about data-driven discussions and budget visibility
- **Claims needing validation:** "Save 1 FTE for every 1x growth in headcount" (marked TBD)

## Lessons & Patterns
- **Extensive copy bank.** The draft framework contains 6+ elevator pitch alternatives, showing how the team iterated on positioning language. Useful as an example of the copy iteration process.
- **Cross-functional adoption is the key challenge.** The framework emphasizes that headcount tools fail when they over-prioritize one persona (HR or Finance). TeamOhana must serve both equally.
- **"Headcount Headache" is memorable.** Alliterative, emotionally resonant, and universally recognized -- a strong example of hero problem naming.
- **Three-axis differentiation** (vs. spreadsheets, vs. platforms, vs. point solutions) is the most granular competitive mapping across all engagements.
- **Scratch space preserved.** The draft shows alternative pillar structures, outcome hierarchies, and naming explorations, providing insight into the creative process.
