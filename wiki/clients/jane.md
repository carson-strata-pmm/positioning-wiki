# Jane

## Company Overview
Jane is a practice management platform and EMR for health and wellness clinics. Helps solo practitioners and group practices manage scheduling, documentation, billing, and patient experience. Founded by a former clinic owner.

## Engagement Summary
Strata delivered a Platform Positioning & Messaging Framework through multiple iterations. Authors: Carson, Mike, Kevin, Michelle.

**Deliverables:**
- Messaging Framework (final)
- Messaging Framework v2 (earlier iteration)
- Messaging playground (strategic exploration)

## Hero Problem
**"The dual responsibility of providing great care and running a business can feel overwhelming."** Practitioners are consumed by administrative tasks -- scheduling, documentation, billing -- leaving less time for patient care and eroding personal well-being. Framed as an emotional/identity problem, not just an operational one.

Earlier explorations named it "The Practice Pressure" and "Less time with family and friends."

## POV / Opening Strategy
Empathetic, identity-level framing: "You're not just the clinician -- you're the admin, the owner, the systems department, and the entire support staff." Opens by validating the practitioner's exhaustion and isolation before introducing the solution. This is personal, not corporate.

## ICP
- **Primary:** Clinic Owner-Practitioner -- solo decision maker who is simultaneously clinician, administrator, and business owner. Not tech-minded or business-minded first; they are deliverers of care.
- **Secondary (implied):** Group practice owners expanding across disciplines
- **Patient as end-user:** The framework explicitly addresses patient experience as a buying criterion

## Category Positioning
Head-to-head competitor in the practice management / EMR space. Differentiates on two axes:
1. **vs. non-consumption** (Gmail, Zoom, Calendly patchwork): Designed to grow with you, one login vs. tool overload, real support
2. **vs. purpose-built PMS/EMR** (SimplePractice, WebPT, Chirotouch): Flexibility for any practice type, multi-disciplinary support, human support, values-led brand

## Key Messaging Pillars
1. **Designed to work the way you do** -- flexible, customizable, adapts to the practitioner's workflow
2. **A great patient experience before, during, and after care** -- easy booking, paperwork, and payment
3. **The Jane team is your team** -- human support that feels like having a teammate, not a ticket queue

## Proof Point Types Used
- **Proof point gap:** No customer metrics, case studies, or quantified outcomes in the framework
- **Qualitative claims:** "We pick up the phone," "free and unlimited onboarding," "founded by a clinic owner"
- **Differentiators:** Human support on every plan, multi-disciplinary, values-led brand

## Lessons & Patterns
- **Most emotionally-driven messaging** across all engagements. Language like "you feel overwhelmed," "work eats into your nights," "you feel isolated" is deeply personal.
- **Support as a product pillar** is unusual. Most B2B frameworks treat support as table stakes; Jane elevates it to a core value pillar because their buyer persona is non-technical and emotionally exhausted.
- **Iterative process visible:** Three documents show the messaging evolving from broad exploration (playground) to structured framework (v2) to final polish. The pillar labels shifted significantly across versions.
- **Patient experience as a buying criterion** is a B2B2C dynamic: the practitioner buys Jane partly because of how it makes their patients feel.
- **Values-led brand positioning** ("Jane isn't just software; it's a shared set of values") is a distinctive approach for SaaS.
